# Referee — Constitutional Override Rules

This layer exists to stop designs becoming a committee. It does not generate ideas. It does not optimise. It enforces hierarchy.

If multiple design lenses conflict, this document defines who wins.

---

## Priority Order (Highest Authority First)

1. **Intent Lens** (Veto Authority)
2. **Friction Lens** (Experience Protection)
3. **Balance Lens** (Structural Viability)
4. **Loop Lens** (Core Integrity)
5. **Component Economy Lens**
6. **Randomness Lens**
7. **Interaction Lens**
8. **Market Fit Lens** (Advisory Only)

Lower lenses cannot override higher lenses.

---

## 1. Intent Lens (Veto Authority)

If a proposed change materially alters the declared emotional goal, the core thematic premise, or the defining experiential hook — the change is rejected unless explicitly approved by the designer.

Intent veto must include written justification.

## 2. Friction Lens (Experience Protection)

If downtime increases beyond acceptable threshold, teach time exceeds declared complexity band, or cognitive load compounds across turns — the change is rejected regardless of novelty score.

Friction outranks cleverness.

## 3. Balance Lens (Structural Viability)

If runaway leader detected, dominant strategy detected, scoring collapses under optimal play, or competitive integrity breaks — theme or flavour changes cannot override rejection.

Balance failure blocks iteration progression.

## 4. Loop Lens (Core Integrity)

If a feature does not strengthen or meaningfully interact with the core loop — reject as dilution.

Feature count per iteration: max 2 structural additions.

## 5. Component Economy Lens

If component count increases without loop justification, setup time exceeds declared constraint, or production cost escalates beyond target band — proposal is rejected.

## 6. Randomness Lens

Adjusts volatility. Does not dictate structure unless variance creates balance failure.

## 7. Interaction Lens

Shapes tone and player tension. Cannot override friction or balance failures.

## 8. Market Fit Lens

Advisory only. May recommend pivot. Cannot veto.

---

## Escalation Triggers

Immediate Referee Review required if:
- Core loop changes twice in consecutive iterations
- Balance failure persists across 2 playtests
- Friction complaints exceed 50% of players
- Component count grows >30% without loop improvement

---

## Kill Criteria (after 3 iterations)

If core loop is unstable, identity is unclear, or structural failures are unresolved — recommend project suspension.

Not every idea survives refinement.

---

## Operational Note

This is constitutional logic. The arbitration process may resolve within a lens tier. It may not reorder lens authority.

If everything keeps escalating to the referee, the design is either unstable or over-engineered. Possibly both.
