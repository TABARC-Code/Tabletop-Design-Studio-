# Playtester Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy to select and interpret simulated tabletop playtesters.

## Experience levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New (<1 yr) | New to tabletop gaming or the specific genre |
| Casual (1–3) | Familiar but not deeply embedded |
| Regular (4–10) | Plays repeatedly and understands conventions |
| Veteran (11–30) | Deep genre knowledge and comparison base |
| Legacy (30+) | Long-term historical perspective, often resistant to novelty but useful for durability |

## Play styles

| Style | Looks for | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Optimiser | Efficiency, combos, strong routes | May overvalue depth and undervalue accessibility |
| Competitive | Fairness, meta, tournament integrity | May punish casual randomness too harshly |
| Narrative | Theme, story, character, emotional arc | May undervalue mechanical elegance |
| Cooperative | Team flow, shared agency | Sensitive to alpha-player problems |
| Social | Laughter, table energy, group inclusion | May undervalue strategic subtlety |
| Explorer | Discovery, variety, novelty | May miss late-game balance problems |
| Chill | Low pressure, calm flow | May resist necessary tension |
| House-ruler | Modularity, flexibility | May normalise unclear rules by patching them |
| Systems analyst | Structure, incentives, edge cases | May sound colder than general audience |
| Teacher | Teachability, onboarding, scaffolding | May focus strongly on first-play clarity |

## Sampling presets

### Balanced

- 25% new/casual.
- 45% regular.
- 30% veteran/legacy.
- Include at least one accessibility lens if tester count permits.
- Include at least one optimiser and one social/narrative player.

### Stress test

- Favour optimisers, competitive players, systems analysts, house-rulers.
- Include high-experience testers.
- Goal: break rules, find dominant strategies, exploit ambiguity.

### Accessibility test

- Include neurodivergent, low-vision, motor-accessibility, fatigue-sensitive, and cognitive-load testers.
- Goal: identify exclusion, strain, ambiguity, overload.

### Market fit

- Match tester types to intended audience.
- Include some adjacent audiences to test expansion potential.

### Blind rules test

- Include new, casual, teacher, and accessibility testers.
- Give only rulebook and components.
- Measure comprehension without designer explanation.
